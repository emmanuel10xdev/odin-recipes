Nigerian Pepper Soup

Nigerian pepper soup is a flavorful and aromatic soup made with meat, fish, or other protein, cooked in a spicy broth infused with traditional spices and fresh herbs.

It is known for its bold peppery taste, rich aroma, and light, warming broth.

To prepare it, the protein is seasoned and cooked with onions, peppers, pepper soup spice, and other desired seasonings until tender and flavorful.

Fresh herbs such as scent leaf or uziza be added toward the end to give the soup its distinctive aroma and taste.

It is best served hot and can be enjoyed on its own or alongside a preferred side dish.

Ingredients

  • Chicken, goat meat, beef, or fish
  • Pepper soup spice
  • Fresh pepper
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Scent leaf or uziza leaves
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Crayfish, ground (optional)
  • Black pepper (optional)

How to Make Nigerian Pepper Soup

  1. Wash and clean your chosen protein thoroughly, then place it in a pot.
  2. Add Chopped onions, fresh pepper, seasoning cubes, and salt.
  3. Add enough water to cover the meat and bring it to boil.
  4. Add pepper soup spice and allow the meat to cook until tender and the broth becomes flavorful.
  5. Taste the broth and adjust the salt, pepper, or seasoning if necessary.
  6. Add chopped scent leaf or uziza and allow it to cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  7. Turn off the heat and serve the pepper soup hot.
