Beans Porridge

Ingredients

  • Brown beans or black-eyed beans
  • Water
  • Onions
  • Palm oil
  • Fresh pepper
  • Tomatoes (optional)
  • Crayfish, ground (optional)
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Smoked fish or dried fish (optional)

How to Make Nigerian Fried Rice

  1. Pick through the beans and remove stones, dirt, and damaged beans.
  2. Wash the beans thoroughly and place them in a pot with enough water.
  3. Boil the beans until they become soft and tender, adding more water when necessary.
  4. Chop the onions and fresh pepper while the beans are cooking.
  5. Once the beans are soft, add the onions, pepper, and tomatoes if using.
  6. Add palm oil, seasoning cubes, and crayfish, then stir thoroughly.
  7. Add smoked or dried fish if desired and allow the beans to simmer for a few minutes.
  8. Add salt to taste and continue cooking until the beans become thick and flavorful.
  9. Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary.
  10. Serve hot with your preferred side, such as fried plantain, bread, or rice.
