Nigerian Fried Rice

Nigerian fried rice is a colorful and flavorful rice dish made with long-grain rice, mixed vegetables, and a variety of seasonings.

It is known for its vibrant appearance, savory taste, and slightly smoky aroma, making it a popular choice for celebrations, family meals, and special occasions.

The dish is prepared by cooking the rice until tender, then stir-frying it with vegetables such as carrots, peas, sweet corn, and green beans.

Seasonings, stock, and a choice of protein such as chicken, beef, or shrimp are added to enhance the flavor.

The ingredients are combined over medium-high heat until the rice is well seasoned and evenly mixed.

Ingredients

  • Long-grain parboiled rice
  • Chicken or other preferred protein
  • Carrots
  • Green peas
  • Sweet corn
  • Green beans
  • Green bell pepper
  • Spring onions
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken stock
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Water
  • Liver, diced (optional)

How to Make Nigerian Fried Rice

  1. Wash and parboil the rice until it is almost tender, then drain and set it aside.
  2. Cook the chicken or preferred protein with onions, seasoning, and salt. Reserve the stock.
  3. Dice the carrots, green beans, bell pepper, and other vegetables into small pieces.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan and sauté the onions until fragrant.
  5. Add the diced vegetables and stir-fry for a few minutes until slightly tender but still crunchy.
  6. Add the cooked chicken or other protein and stir to combine.
  7. Add the parboiled rice and mix thoroughly with the vegetables and protein.
  8. Add chicken stock, curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes, and salt to taste.
  9. Stir-fry everything over medium-high heat until the rice is fully cooked and evenly coated with the seasonings.
  10. Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary, then serve hot.
